Pawn earning rates.

Headless runs played by a bot on the player's side, counted per run. The seconds start at the owner's own pace, measured from a 16-run session on 2026-11-17. Every rate below is recomputed from those counts with the pawn values and time assumptions on the left, so a price or timing change needs no new games.

Targets

The owner's targets, against prep-only drilling (all levels, both sides, at the owner's pace) as 1×. Rates for full runs and Middlegame use a new player's pace past prep. Held the draw is not in the runs, so it isn't checked.

1 · Every strategy, ranked

One row per strategy, bot level and side. The rate pools each group: total pawns over total minutes.

2 · Prep only

Play the prepared moves, add growth's top answer when growth opens, end the run with the X, start the next.

3 · Full runs, broken down

Drill runs played to their end, without misses. Run length counts your moves.

4 · Runs that end in checkmate

Full runs (same filters) where you gave mate, against the rest.

5 · Where the pawns come from

Share of each strategy's pawns by event, at the values on the left. The streak pays on top of these, once a day.

6 · Endgames by family

Endgames pays bounties, not per round: first clear, first mission and mastered once per ending, then a tune-up when one comes due (14 days after mastery, doubling to 90). Mastered is 3 successes in a row on different days, so time to master assumes 3 rounds at the measured success rate. The bot plays the engine's best move.

7 · As the Playbook grows

Full White Drill runs at each level, grouped by how far into the session they came. Growth adds moves as runs go by.

8 · Price sheet

Proposed prices, editable here. Time is shown three ways: minutes of prep-only drilling at the baseline, and hours of typical full runs (1200, White) at the owner's pace and at a new player's pace past prep. Packs show what their pawns would take to earn. The streak is left out.
